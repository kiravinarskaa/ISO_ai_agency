## Recommendations

1. **Confirm the mandate and scope.** Obtain client confirmation of the legal entity, accountable decision makers, intended business services, organizational boundaries, locations, interfaces, dependencies, and target deliverables. Record scope approval and exclusion rationale. Treat every intake claim as unverified until supported by appropriate source records.
2. **Complete discovery and evidence collection.** Build and validate an asset and service inventory, data and system flows, supplier/cloud dependencies, applicable obligations, and existing governance and operational documentation. Identify evidence owners with the client; do not represent unprovided material as reviewed.
3. **Establish the ISMS planning basis.** With the client, define context, interested parties, obligations, risk assessment criteria and method, and risk treatment approach. Assess risks using client-approved criteria, then document treatment decisions and the Statement of Applicability where applicable. These are proposed activities, not findings that work has already occurred.
4. **Assess implementation and operating evidence.** Map applicable requirements to documented responsibilities and evidence. For each area, distinguish designed, implemented, and operating status only when client evidence supports it; otherwise record it as unknown or missing. Review training, access, change, backup, logging, supplier, incident, and continuity evidence only if supplied and in scope.
5. **Plan remediation and governance.** Prioritize gaps using client-approved criteria, assign proposed owners and target dates only after client agreement, and track actions, dependencies, evidence needed, and acceptance decisions. Establish client-approved monitoring, internal audit, and management review plans as appropriate.
6. **Use the eight-week target as a provisional sequence, not a promised completion date.** After kickoff is agreed, sequence weeks 1–2 for mandate, scope, and discovery; weeks 3–4 for risk and obligation analysis; weeks 5–6 for evidence review and gap/action planning; and weeks 7–8 for client review, unresolved-evidence tracking, and a readiness checkpoint. Re-plan with the client if access, evidence, scope, or decisions make this sequence infeasible. This is a proposed sequence only; it does not assert work dates or completion.
7. **Keep the conclusion bounded.** Any later readiness summary should state its evidence base, limitations, unresolved items, and review date. This plan makes no certification or audit-conformity claim; certification decisions belong to an independent certification body.

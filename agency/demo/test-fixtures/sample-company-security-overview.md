# Northstar Sample Foods - fictional test company profile

> **TEST DATA ONLY.** Northstar Sample Foods and all people, locations, systems, and facts in this document are fictional. Do not use as evidence about a real organization.

## Company and assessment request

- **Organization:** Northstar Sample Foods (fictional)
- **Industry:** Food distribution
- **Employees:** Approximately 42
- **Operating locations:** One office and warehouse in Tashkent, Uzbekistan
- **Objective:** Obtain a preliminary ISO/IEC 27001 readiness assessment, identify documentation and control gaps, and receive a prioritized improvement roadmap.
- **Proposed scope:** Corporate IT and information handling that support order processing, supplier management, invoicing, and warehouse operations. The assessment should first confirm whether the warehouse systems and any outsourced IT services belong in scope.

## Systems in the proposed scope

- Cloud-hosted business email and calendars
- Cloud file storage used for contracts, supplier records, invoices, and operating procedures
- A hosted accounting and invoicing service
- An inventory and order-management application used by office and warehouse staff
- Approximately 42 company laptops and 6 shared warehouse workstations
- Office and warehouse Wi-Fi and network equipment
- An external IT support provider with administrative access to some systems

## Information handled

- Customer names, business contact details, and order records
- Supplier contacts, contracts, and pricing documents
- Employee records and work contact details
- Invoices and internal operating procedures

## Security measures reported by the fictional company

- Individual accounts for most staff
- Password-based sign-in; multifactor authentication is enabled for cloud email but its coverage has not been checked
- Endpoint protection on company-managed computers
- Cloud file sharing with role-based folders, though access is not reviewed on a fixed schedule
- Backups provided by cloud services; no recent restore-test record is available
- Informal process for reporting suspicious email and IT issues
- External IT provider handles routine support and selected administrative tasks

## Known gaps to validate

- No consolidated inventory of information assets, owners, and suppliers
- No documented organization-wide information security risk assessment or approved risk treatment plan
- No documented periodic user access review
- No formal supplier security review process
- No complete security-awareness training record
- No internal ISMS audit or management review record
- No evidence of a recent backup restoration exercise
- The boundary between the accounting, order-management, warehouse, and outsourced IT services has not been formally agreed
